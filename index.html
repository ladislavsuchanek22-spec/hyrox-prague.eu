How we train for HYROX

8 km. 8 stations. Nothing random.

We train every segment on its own and all together. Transitions included.

02Sled Push50 m
03Sled Pull50 m
04Burpee Broad Jump80 m
05Rowing1 000 m
06Farmers Carry200 m
07Sandbag Lunges100 m
08Wall Balls100 reps

1 km run before every station.

Base first

Before we add speed, we build capacity. Skip the basics and you'll pay for it in the second half of the race.

Performance under fatigue

Wall balls after 7 km are a different sport than fresh ones. We learn to hold technique and pace when it hurts.

The right dose

More isn't always better. There's a time to build, to push and to recover. We manage fatigue so you don't lose your season.

HYROX training FAQ

What people ask.

What is HYROX?

HYROX is a fitness race: 8 × 1 km of running, with one of 8 functional stations after every kilometre (SkiErg, sled push, sled pull, burpee broad jumps, rowing, farmers carry, sandbag lunges, wall balls). The format is the same worldwide, so times are comparable.

Is it for beginners?

Yes. It doesn't matter where you start, only that you show up and do the work. For a first race, Basic or Race Prep is ideal.

How do I get my training?

A plan for every week with pacing, sets and the purpose of each session. Questions go through WhatsApp.

What equipment do I need?

Ideally a SkiErg, rower, sled and wall ball. If something's missing, we adjust the session.

How long should I prepare for my first HYROX?

Depends on where you start. If you already run and train regularly, 8–12 weeks of focused prep is usually enough for a first race. Starting from zero, plan for a few months.

What if I miss a session?

Let me know and we'll rearrange the week. What you do over months matters, not one day.

Do I need to live in Prague?

No. I coach athletes online anywhere. If you're in Prague, Brno or Ostrava, you can join the clubs too.

How long is the commitment?

Basic and Advanced run month to month, Race Prep is 8 weeks. Real progress still takes time.
